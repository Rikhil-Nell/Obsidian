## 1. **Joining Bonus Coupon**

- **Offer:** ₹75 OFF on first order above ₹299
    
- **Valid for:** 14 days from signup
    
- **Type:** Dine-in & takeaway only (not valid with other offers)
    

### Reasoning:

- AOV is ₹303.57 → ₹75 off is ~25% discount → appealing but margin-protective.
    
- ₹299 minimum ensures upsell and avoids low-value redemptions.
    
- 14-day expiry creates urgency.
    
- Dine-in/takeaway avoids platform commission, builds direct customer habits.
    

### Cost Analysis:

- **New customers:** 216
    
- **Expected redemption rate:** 30% → ~65 customers
    
- **Incremental sales:** ₹305 × 65 = ₹19,825
    
- **Discount cost:** ₹75 × 65 = ₹4,875
    
- **Net profit:** ₹4,956 – ₹4,875 = ₹81
    
- **Verdict:** Break-even, but solid for acquisition. ROI improves if >30% repeat.

## 2. **Stamp Card Coupon**

- **Offer:** Buy 5, Get ₹150 OFF on 6th order (≥ ₹299)
    
- **Valid for:** 60 days
    
- **Type:** Dine-in & takeaway only (not valid with other offers)
    

### Reasoning:

- Active users average 1.85 orders → incentivizes stretch to 6.
    
- ₹150 reward (~50% of AOV) is strong loyalty motivator.
    
- 60 days = enough time to complete + urgency.
    
- Minimum order value protects margins; dine-in focus again reduces platform costs.
    

### Cost Analysis:

- **Active customers:** 98
    
- **Expected participation:** 50% = 49 users
    
- **Completion rate:** 40% = 20 users
    
- **Incremental orders:** 20 × 5 = 100 orders
    
- **Revenue uplift:** ₹305 × 100 = ₹30,500
    
- **Discount cost:** 20 × ₹150 = ₹3,000
    
- **Net profit:** ₹7,625 – ₹3,000 = ₹4,625
    
- **Break-even:** Only 8 completions
    
- **Verdict:** High ROI, strengthens loyalty, easy to scale.

## 3. **Miss You Coupon**

- **Offer:** ₹100 OFF on order above ₹299
    
- **Sent after:** 60 days of inactivity
    
- **Valid for:** 14 days
    
- **Type:** Dine-in & takeaway only (not valid with other offers)
    

### Reasoning:

- Dormant user base: 4,869 users, avg spend ₹304.52
    
- ₹100 discount = ~33% → strong reactivation hook.
    
- 14-day expiry forces quick re-engagement.
    
- 60-day inactivity aligns with churn behavior.
    
- Dine-in only = cost-saving, deeper relationship-building.
    

### Cost Analysis:

- **Expected redemption:** 18% = 876 users
    
- **Incremental sales:** ₹305 × 876 = ₹267,180
    
- **Discount cost:** ₹100 × 876 = ₹87,600
    
- **Net profit:** ₹66,795 – ₹87,600 = **–₹20,805** (short-term loss)
    
- **Break-even if:**
    
    - Redemptions ≥ 1,170 _or_
        
    - 20% of reactivated customers return again
        
- **Verdict:** Loss-leader, but with long-term CLV upside.
