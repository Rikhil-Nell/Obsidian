# Giga Rejection

## What They Wanted

- New-grad engineers who can be in San Francisco, in-office, five days a week, now
- No remote, no exceptions — stated explicitly in the application
- US-based or immediately relocatable candidates

---

## What I Had

- Strong production engineering profile (voice AI, agent backends)
- Honest disclosure: India-based, final year, post-grad relocation intent, sponsorship needed
- No ability to be in SF in-office on their timeline

---

## Missing Signals

### Likely

- **Geography / immediacy** — the only filter that mattered. They need bodies in the SF office now. Post-grad-2027 relocation does not fit a new-grad role they want to fill soon.
- **Sponsorship + not-currently-in-US** — added friction on top of the timeline.

### Unlikely

- Engineering ability. Like Periodic, this was not a skills evaluation. The "anything else important" disclosure likely routed it to a fast no on logistics.

---

## Did I Actually Want This Job?

Interest Rating: 6/10

Comp and intensity were attractive. Product thesis was never even clear from the apply flow. Wanted the option more than the specific job.

---

## Was This A Fair Rejection?

Reason: **Fair, but the "unprecedented" sting is worth examining.** The Giga note predicted a geography filter and called it a likely hard block. So on paper, expected.

Why it stung anyway (vs Periodic, which didn't): Periodic was a credential gate I knew I didn't clear. Giga was a *geography* gate — and geography feels more arbitrary than credentials because it has nothing to do with whether I can do the work. It is the same nerve the YC SF expo hit: "you'd be a fit if you were just physically there." Being filtered for where you were born/located, repeatedly, accumulates. That is the real source of the surprise — not that the logic was wrong, but that the honest disclosure got a fast no when part of me hoped the work would override the location.

It didn't, because for a now-hiring in-office new-grad role, it can't. That is rational on their end and frustrating on mine. Both true.

---

## What Did This Teach Me?

### Technical

- Nothing tested. No technical gap.

### Career

- **In-office-now US roles are a structural dead end from India, full stop** — regardless of profile strength. Stop spending emotional energy on them; treat them as pure lottery tickets like SpaceX, not as real prospects.
- The roles that can actually convert: remote / no-visa (Crustdata), India-based (DualEntry, Peakflo, Emergent), or future-dated with sponsorship (Circleback Summer 2027). Weight the pipeline toward those.
- Disclosing early was still correct — it saved a wasted loop. The fast no is the system working, not a failure of pitch.
