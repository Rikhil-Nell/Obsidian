## What Did This Teach Me?

### Technical

- Nothing tested. No new gap surfaced.

### Career

- **Assignment-mandatory ≠ respect for your time.** Even YC startups with explicit process can close roles mid-flight and send a one-line template.
- **The app is not sunk cost** — if it's good, it's portfolio/signal. Ship it publicly; the work counts even when the process doesn't.
- **Before heavy take-homes:** check if role still appears on their site / Ashby; for small teams, a one-line "still actively hiring for this seat?" can save days (optional, not always answered).
- Pattern: India YC paths can respond fast — but "closed role" is a different failure mode than ghosting. Still zero feedback on work quality.
