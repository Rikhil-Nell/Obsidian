## Interesting Problems

### Information Architecture for LLMs

Legacy paradigms (wikis, KBs, search UIs) assume human navigation. LLMs need different organization, retrieval, and interaction patterns. Greenfield systems design.

### Teaching Models New Architectures

Finetuning and RL so models learn to use novel information structures, not bolt-on RAG hacks.

### Hard Knowledge Base Evals

Building eval sets that surface failure modes when models work with external data. Measurement over demo impressiveness.

### Agentic Search

Advanced search capabilities where agents reason over retrieved information, not single-shot retrieval.
