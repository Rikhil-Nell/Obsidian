## Interesting Problems

### Multi-Agent Population Simulation

Core product: generate thousands of AI agents that mirror human behavior, simulate entire populations, predict how demographics/geographies respond to events, products, policies, campaigns. Agent-based prediction — not surveys, not focus groups. Structurally: orchestration + state + identity + behavioral models at population scale.

### Platform as Leverage Layer

Platform engineer role sits between research/simulation tech and customer-facing product. Build abstractions so complex simulation capabilities become usable by customers and other agents. Framework-level code that absorbs complexity — exactly the "layer beneath the application" instinct.

### Prediction at Real-World Stakes

Public proof point: accurately predicted NY Democratic primary. Clients: Accenture, EY, Interpublic Group, political campaigns. IPG partnership for AI-powered consumer simulations before campaigns launch. High correctness bar — wrong predictions have real consequences.

### Whole World Simulation Thesis

Founders describe products as "puzzle pieces to building whole world simulation." Multi-agent approach rooted in transactionalism — value created through dynamic interactions between parties. Ambitious infrastructure thesis, not a wrapper.

### Product Innovation Speed

Partners use Aaru to cut product innovation timelines from months to minutes, deploy marketing that wins when consumers are harder to understand, refine strategy in volatile geopolitical climates.
