## On Hacker News being "too early"

Your worry — cashflow, client stability — is legitimate for the rawest end of HN. But you're half-misdiagnosing it, and here's the important part:

You actually _want_ early-stage. Your own fit profile says it: early-stage, high ownership, founder proximity, infrastructure-first. The thing you're scared of isn't "early" — it's "early _and unfunded_," two people with no runway and no customers. Those are different axes.

This is exactly what VC/accelerator boards solve. They give you early-stage energy _with a funding floor_. A company on Lightspeed's or a16z's board has raised real money from people who diligenced it. You get the ownership and velocity you want, minus the "will payroll clear next month" risk that makes raw HN feel sketchy. That's the synthesis: early-stage + named investor = your fit profile without the cashflow gamble.

So don't abandon HN — re-read it with a filter. The "Who is Hiring" thread has plenty of Series A/B and profitable bootstrapped companies mixed in with the two-person stuff. Ctrl-F "remote," "intern," "Series A," skip anyone who can't name funding or revenue. But spend the _bulk_ of your energy on the VC boards, because they're pre-filtered to the funded tier you're actually comfortable with.

## The workflow

1. Pick 3–4 VC boards (start: a16z, Lightspeed, General Catalyst, AI Grant)
2. Filter Remote + Engineering/Intern
3. Cross-reference interesting ones against the curated lists for traction signal
4. Apply on the company site; for the best ones, also DM the founder (Sales Navigator while you still have it)
5. Log in the CRM, then forget it — silence is the default, remember
